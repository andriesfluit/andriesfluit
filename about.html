About

I'm Andries Fluit, an amateur photographer based in Belgium. My work explores quiet moments found in travel and documentary photography.

Through my lens, I seek to capture the poetry in everyday scenes: the interplay of light and shadow, the textures of unfamiliar places, and the fleeting expressions that reveal something deeper about the human experience.

My photographs have taken me from the misty volcanic landscapes of Madeira to the ancient streets of Shiraz, from the highlands of Madagascar to the Arctic fjords of Norway. Each journey offers new perspectives and stories waiting to be told.

If you're interested in prints or have any questions, please don't hesitate to get in touch.
